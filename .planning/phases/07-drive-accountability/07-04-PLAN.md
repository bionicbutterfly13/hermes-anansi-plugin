# Plan 07-04: Containment Completion + Consolidated Fail-Open Matrix

## Objective

Close the remaining DRIVE-06 containment surface and the fail-open matrix. 07-01 landed the SEPARATE
drive kill switch (control 1 of 3). This plan adds control 2 (a domain whitelist — drive operates
only in user-named domains) and control 3 (a per-turn energy/attention budget — a hard cap on how
many drive lines surface), both resolving from `plugins.entries.anansi` config and both fail-open to
safe defaults. It also consolidates every new drive path into the SAFE-02 fail-open matrix (one row
per case, the project's standing pattern) and documents all three containment controls in the plugin
README. The never-omit invariant (DRIVE-05) takes precedence over the energy budget: a flagged-
priority want is never dropped to satisfy the budget.
